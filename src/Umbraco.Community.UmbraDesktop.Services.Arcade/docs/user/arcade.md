# Arcade

The Arcade keeps your best score in each game on the desktop and ranks everyone's on leaderboards.
It arrives with a game that uses it, such as UmbraDesktop Entertainment. You do not install it
yourself.

## Open the Arcade

To see the leaderboards, open the launcher and select **Arcade** in the Games group. The Arcade is
not there until a game that uses it is installed.

The hub has a tab for each game, and a board for each way of ranking that game. Solitaire has one
board for draw one and one for draw three. Each board lists the top ten players with their rank,
display name, score and date. A time under a minute shows as seconds to one decimal, and a longer one
as minutes and seconds.

Your own row is highlighted. If you are outside the top ten, or your scores are private, it is shown
beneath the list, marked **Private: only you see this** when that is the case. A board nobody has
played says so.

To play a game, select **Play** on its tab. The game opens in its own window.

## Your first score

The first time you finish a game that keeps score, the Arcade asks **Show your scores on the Arcade?**

- Select **Show them** to appear on the leaderboards.
- Select **Keep them private** to keep your scores to yourself. The Arcade still keeps them, and you
  still see them.

The question is asked once. You can change your mind at any time in the **Profile** tab.

When a score is your best on a board, a notification says so, with your rank when your scores are shown. A score that is not
your best raises nothing.

## Your profile

To change how you appear, select the **Profile** tab.

- **Display name** is the name on the leaderboards. It starts as your name in Umbraco.
- **Show my scores on the leaderboards** turns your appearance on the boards on or off.
- **Tell me when somebody takes first place from you**: when it is on, the desktop tells you after
  somebody takes first place on a board you led. The notification appears the next time the desktop
  opens, and only for people who show their scores.

To save a change, select **Save**.

## Delete your scores

To remove everything the Arcade holds about you, select **Delete my scores** on the **Profile** tab,
then confirm. Your scores and Arcade settings are removed from every board. This cannot be undone.

Playing again afterwards starts you fresh, with your scores private. The Arcade asks the first-score question again the next time you open the desktop.

## What administrators can do

A user with the **Users** section can, on any board, select **Remove** to take a score off it, select
**Reset board** to remove every score on it, and **Reset name** to give a player their Umbraco name
back. The Arcade cannot tell a real score from one sent by hand, so this is how junk gets cleaned up.

## When somebody leaves

A colleague whose account is disabled or locked drops off the boards, and returns if the account is
enabled again. A deleted account takes its scores with it.

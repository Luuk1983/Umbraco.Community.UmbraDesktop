# Paint

Paint has a pencil, a brush, an eraser and fill, MS Paint's own twenty-eight colours, and Undo. It
works on a new picture or on an image opened from the media library.

- To choose a colour, select it in the palette: the left button sets the foreground colour, the right
  button the background colour.
- To draw, choose **Pencil**, **Brush**, **Eraser** or **Fill**, and draw on the picture.
- To make the picture bigger or smaller, drag the handles on its edges, or focus a handle and use the
  arrow keys.
- To take back a change, select **Undo**.

Paint opens pictures up to 4,096 pixels across, at their own size, and saves each in the format it
came in: a JPEG stays a JPEG. It refuses an SVG, which it could only flatten into pixels and would
destroy by saving.

Undo keeps as many steps as fit in one memory budget shared by every Paint window, so a large picture
keeps fewer steps than a small one.

Opening and saving work the same way as in Notepad. See [Opening and saving files](media-files.md).

# Calculator

Calculator is the Windows Standard calculator without its scientific row. It works left to right, as
a pocket calculator does, and shows `0.3` for `0.1 + 0.2`.

Use the keypad in the window, or the keyboard: the digits, `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `%`, `.`, Enter or
`=` for equals, Backspace, Delete to clear the entry, and Escape to clear everything.

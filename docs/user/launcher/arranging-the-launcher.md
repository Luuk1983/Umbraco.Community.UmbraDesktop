# Arranging the launcher

Until it is changed, the launcher is the one the catalogue builds: every app in the group its
catalogue puts it in, nothing hidden. Everything on this page is optional.

A package that adds apps keeps working the same way after the launcher has been arranged: a new app
still turns up in its group by itself.

## Drag a tile

Dragging works straight from the launcher, without entering Arrange.

- To move an app, drag its tile onto another tile to place it before or after that one, or onto a
  group's empty space to add it there.
- To pin an app, drag its tile onto **Pinned**. An empty Pinned appears at the top of the launcher as
  soon as a drag starts, even when nothing is pinned yet.
- To take an app off the launcher, drag its tile onto the remove pane, which covers the footer for as
  long as the drag lasts.

Nothing is deleted. A removed app still shows up in **All apps**, and in Arrange's list of what is
missing.

## Arrange

To edit groups, select **Arrange**, next to **All apps**. A banner says the launcher is being
arranged, and tiles do not open while it is.

In Arrange:

- To rename a group, edit its name.
- To create a group, select **New group**.
- To delete a group, open its ⋯ menu and select **Delete group**. Its apps are not lost: they wait
  under **Not on your launcher**.
- To move a group, drag its handle, use the arrow keys once the handle has focus, or use the options
  in its ⋯ menu.
- To move an app to another group or to Pinned, drag it, or select its **Move to** button and pick
  the destination.
- To reorder apps within a group, drag them, or use the arrow keys once a tile has focus.
- To take an app off the launcher, select its **Remove from launcher** button.
- To put an app back, select it under **Not on your launcher**. To put a whole group back, select
  **Add group**.
- To undo every group edit, select **Reset to default** and confirm. The groups go back to the way the
  catalogue built them. Pins are left alone.

When finished, select **Done**. Arrange stays open until then, so a click that misses does not throw
you out of it.

In the narrow themes, Windows 98 and Umbraco 4, the launcher widens while arranging, so the list of
what is missing sits beside it.

## After upgrading

An app now lives in one place, so on upgrade your pinned apps (by default the Content editor, the
Media library and the Log Viewer) move out of their groups and show under Pinned only. Otherwise the
launcher stays as it ships until it is arranged.

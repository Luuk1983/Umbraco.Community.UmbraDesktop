# The welcome wizard

The first time you open the desktop, it says welcome and asks three things: your language, your
themes, and whether the desktop should open when you sign in. Every question is already answered with a
default, so selecting **Next** on each page and **Done** on the last one finishes it.

## Go through the wizard

1. Wait a moment on the welcome screen, or select anywhere or press any key to go on. The language
   page opens.
2. To change the language, select it in the list. The backoffice and the wizard switch to it
   straight away. Select **Next**.
3. To change the look of the desktop around your windows, select one of the five under
   **Desktop theme**. Each comes with its own wallpaper.
4. Optional: to change the look inside every window, select one under **Backoffice theme**:
   Umbraco's own light, dark or high contrast. It applies straight away.
5. Select **Next**.
6. Optional: to open the desktop every time you sign in, turn on **Open the desktop when I sign in**.
   It takes effect the next time you open the backoffice.
7. Select **Done**. The wizard fades out over the desktop in the theme you chose.

To go back a page, select **Back**, bottom left.

## Languages the desktop shows in English

The list holds every language the backoffice is available in. The desktop itself is translated into
fewer, and a language it is not translated into ends its row in the desktop icon and **English**.
Hover over the mark to see what it means: with that language chosen, the backoffice in every window
uses it, and the desktop around the windows stays in English.

## Change your answers later

The wizard does not come back once it is finished. Everything it asks is in Desktop settings:

| To change | See |
| --- | --- |
| The language | [Language and region](../settings/language-and-region.md) |
| The theme or the wallpaper | [Themes](../appearance/themes.md) and [Wallpaper](../appearance/wallpaper.md) |
| The backoffice theme | [Backoffice theme](../appearance/backoffice-colours.md) |
| Opening the desktop when you sign in | [Starting in the desktop](../settings/starting-in-the-desktop.md) |

## Who sees it

The wizard shows to anybody who has no desktop settings yet, on their account or in this browser.
Your settings are kept [on your account](../settings/settings-on-your-account.md), so a second
browser or computer does not show it again.

Closing the tab before selecting **Done** saves nothing but the language, and the wizard shows again
the next time. If your account cannot be reached when the desktop opens, the wizard waits for a time
when it can, since it could not save your answers.

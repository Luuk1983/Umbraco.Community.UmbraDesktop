The default deck

Pips, indices and the branded ace of spades are as designed. The court figures here are placeholders: the real ones are Dmitry Fomin's CC0 English pattern courts, recoloured in the Umbraco palette (design D8). An HQ deck can follow later as a second face set.

Backs and felt follow the theme

Each theme gets its own back and table colour. Settings let a player pick any back regardless of theme, and other packages can add backs of their own.

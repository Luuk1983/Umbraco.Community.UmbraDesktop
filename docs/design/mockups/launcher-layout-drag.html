Launcher, third pass: Pinned is a place, and tiles drag without Arrange

Each app has exactly one spot on the launcher: Pinned or a group. All apps is always the complete list. Dragging a tile works in normal mode; a click still launches.

1 · Pinned as a place

Content editor, Media library and Log Viewer are pinned, so they are no longer in Editing and Diagnostics. Unpinning with nowhere else to go sends an app back to its catalogue group, by the same rule that places new apps.

2 · Dragging in normal mode

Health Check is lifted out of Diagnostics (its spot shows faded) and is over Pinned, which highlights with an insertion slot. The footer is replaced by the remove pane for as long as the drag lasts. Dropping on another tile or group moves it there. Only apps move here; groups, rename, delete and the palette stay in Arrange.

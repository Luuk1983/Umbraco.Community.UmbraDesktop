UmbraDesktop · Entertainment package · design mock

umbraCoffee, redesigned

A proper player for the monthly community live stream, native to each theme. This page is a static mock: every state is drawn below, top to bottom. Nothing here is built yet.

What changes compared to today

  • YouTube's own controls are switched off. Our control bar sits under the picture and looks like the theme.
  • The picture stays YouTube's embedded player, and we never draw on top of it.
  • A header says what is on, whether it is live, and who is hosting.
  • Chat and Episodes share one side panel with tabs, so the player keeps its size.
  • Before a stream there is a real countdown with the date and time, and a way to watch the last episode.
  • Episodes get thumbnails and dates instead of a plain text list.
  • Each theme gets a player in its own style, from Windows Media Player 6.4 to QuickTime.
  • The same player will play local files in the Video Player app later.

Rules the design keeps. YouTube's picture is 16:9 and never smaller than 200 by 200. Nothing of ours goes over it: no overlay controls, no gradients, no badges. YouTube may still show its own title and logo inside its frame, and the mock draws that hint so it stays honest.

1

Umbraco theme: live, upcoming, on demand

The default theme, drawn with the backoffice's own UUI values: Lato, white boxes on the light grey ground, the navy primary button, 3px corners. The window opens at 640 by 360 for the player plus 320 for the side panel, as today.

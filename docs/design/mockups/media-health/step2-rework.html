1 · Overview, wide and narrow

Three cards when there is room, three rows when there is not

  • One layout that follows the window's width, not two screens. From about 900 px the kinds are cards side by side, each with its figure large, its line, four thumbnails and See all. Below that they are compact rows, without the thumbnail strips (they were the part that sprawled at full screen and cramped at 640).
  • Where the space goes sits beside the cards when it fits, under them when it does not, and lists more folders the more room it has.
  • A folder name opens the folder breakdown (scene 2), not the cleanup list. Browse all folders opens its top level.
  • Default window size goes up to about 960 by 720; at that size the wide layout already applies.

2 · Where the space goes

A folder breakdown you click into

  • What Luuk expected: click a folder and see where its space goes. Like macOS Storage or WinDirStat, one level at a time: the folder's own bar, then each folder under it, largest first, with a bar in the same colours and what in it can go.
  • Clicking a row goes one level down; the path at the top goes back up. Open in Media Manager opens the folder you are in.
  • The tags and the buttons under the table open the cleanup list (scene 3) for this folder and everything under it, so the two views meet without being the same view.
  • This replaces a tree for unused items: the "where" lives here, the lists stay "what can go".

3 · The list, with details beside it

A table first, like core's own table view

  • The list is a table: a small thumbnail, the whole name, folder, size, last changed. Grouped by folder, the group row carries the group's checkbox, count and size. No card view (Luuk, 2026-10-29): the pane shows the picture large.
  • Clicking a row makes it the current one and shows it in the details pane on the right: the picture, the facts, the actions. Nothing covers the list, so a stray click costs nothing. Ticking the checkbox selects, as in core.
  • The pane is the desktop's own attached pane (docs/developer/attached-windows.md), the one Preview, the views beside the editor and the Media Manager's item use: docked behind a splitter with its header, pop out and close, or floating as a window grouped with this one. Nothing new is drawn for it.
  • One by one stays: the panel's action works on the current row and moves to the next, and Previous / Next walk the list.
  • The selection bar is core's own, the navy bar the Media Manager shows, with "214 of 733 selected" and the tab's action. Keep… and the Kept tab arrive with step 3.
  • Where the window has no room to widen, the desktop opens the pane floating, as it does for every attached pane.

4 · Drag to select

Draw a box over the rows

  • Pressing on an empty part of a row (not the name, not the checkbox) and dragging draws a box; every row it touches is selected while you drag, as in Explorer and Finder. Releasing keeps them.
  • Dragging down the checkbox column does the same.
  • Near the top or bottom edge the list scrolls along. The box is the Media Manager's own (selection/box.ts and its element), not a second one.
  • A click without a drag only makes the row current; it never selects or clears.

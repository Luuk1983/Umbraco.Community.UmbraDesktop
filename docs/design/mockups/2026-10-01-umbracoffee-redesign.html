UmbraDesktop, Entertainment package, design mock round 2

umbraCoffee, calmer

Every state is drawn below as a full window at its real size. Nothing here is built yet. The Umbraco theme is drawn with the backoffice's own values: Lato, 33px fields and buttons, 3px corners, the same greys and blues.

What changed and why

  • One scroll area. Only the episode list scrolls; the window never does.
  • No cards. The player runs edge to edge, our controls sit right under it, and the episode details sit on the window itself.
  • Nothing empty. An episode without a summary or guests shows its title and date and stops there.
  • One search field for title, topic and guest. The guest dropdown and the count line are gone; a count only appears while you search.
  • The header is gone. Live, Upcoming or Latest episode is a small tag on the date line under the title.
  • Remind me moved into Settings, behind a gear at the top right, like Solitaire.
  • Offline is a warning icon with a tooltip instead of a banner.
  • Every theme shows every control. Only the look changes.

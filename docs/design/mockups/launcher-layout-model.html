Launcher, second pass: today's launcher by default, arranging is subtraction

Same admin, every package installed. Nobody has to build anything: the launcher is always built from the catalogue, and only your changes to it are stored. New apps land in their catalogue group automatically, before and after you arrange.

1 · Your launcher, never arranged

Exactly today's launcher, package groups included, minus the pin badge. Nothing trimmed, so no opt-out list and no upgrade surprise. Two new controls on the search row: All apps and Arrange. No new-apps badge: new apps simply appear in their group.

3 · Arrange, for someone who has trimmed

This user deleted Advanced security, removed a few tools, and made their own Daily group. The palette on the right holds only what is not on the launcher, grouped by catalogue category: Add group brings a deleted group back whole, Add all puts the missing apps back in their group, + adds one. Deleting a group sends its apps here, so it needs no confirm. Profiling is mid-drag back into Diagnostics.

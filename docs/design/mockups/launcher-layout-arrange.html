Launcher, fourth pass: arrange mode without dragging

No right-click anywhere. Everything a drag does has a visible button in arrange mode, where tiles do not launch, so a button on a tile cannot be hit by accident.

Arrange, with Move to open on Data Types

Every tile: − removes it from the launcher (it goes to the palette), ⋯ opens Move to: Pinned, every other group, New group, and Remove. That list is an inline popover inside the panel, not a modal. A focused tile moves within its group with the arrow keys. Group headers: handle (drag, or focus and arrow keys), rename field, delete (apps go to the palette). Pinned is a place like a group, but fixed: it cannot be renamed, moved or deleted, because the taskbar reads it too. The palette on the right holds what is not on the launcher, with +, Add all and Add group.
